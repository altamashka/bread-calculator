Build reusable steps for autolyse, folds, fermentation and baking. Recipes and bake logs keep the method version they were saved with.

Method steps

Add each action in order. “Rest after” is the wait before starting the next step. Enter 0 to continue straight away. Optionally assign ingredients to the step where you weigh and add them. Percentages refer to each ingredient’s full recipe quantity; split water across steps if needed. Any remainder stays in optional preparation.

Planned schedule

    The schedule adds up your rest times; hands-on work takes extra time. It does not predict when fermentation will finish. Check dough condition and record actual times in the bake log.

    Using a method

    Choose it in the calculator before saving a recipe. When saving in Mealie, confirm the method you want to include. Changing a method here does not rewrite recipes or past bake results.
