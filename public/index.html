Calculation notes

Flour absorption varies. Whole grain flours usually absorb more water. Flour strength, milling, fermentation and handling also change how dough feels. Keep notes rather than chasing a single ideal percentage.

Percentage basis. The water input is a percentage of added flour. The large hydration figure counts all flour and water, including starter. Milk appears in a separate liquid hydration figure. Eggs, butter, oil and honey affect dough but are excluded from both figures.

Use a scale. Grams avoid the uncertainty of flour packed into cups. We retain full precision in the calculation and round the display to 0.1 g. Timings and temperatures belong in your method; this calculator does not predict fermentation.
