How the calculations work

Ingredient percentages use added flour. Total hydration includes all flour and water in the starter. Milk appears in a separate liquid hydration figure; eggs, butter, oil and honey are excluded.

Flour absorption varies with grain, milling and strength. Keep notes about how your dough behaves. We retain full calculation precision and display weights to 0.1 g.

Methods contain planned steps and rests. Temperature and starter activity affect fermentation; use dough condition as well as the clock.
